22 February 2025 - degrading mental state

Nonstop suffering from worsening state of life. Is there a way out..?

Read more

I went to a mental clinic on Friday to have my mental health checked up. After a talk with a young doctor, I was diagnosed with ADHD... and depression as secondary effect. I am now on medication - antidepressants first, then ADHD-related. Though I am not sure if this has to stop when I would move out next year.

I was meant to update my webpage earlier, but now I will write here: jobhunt is in progress. Though I am cooling off with it briefly to review all the tasks that I need to tackle, and try to lighten depression. Medication might help me with these issues, but there is no guarantee. I have been demotivated for years now, and I finally realise the consequences of skipping and delaying important tasks. I am quite doomed.

In near future, I want to: find a workplace to hire me, move out on my own to a flat/apartment room, and aspire to migrate to northern America or Europe. Details are not confirmed - these can be arranged.

Really, I honestly do not care if I don't get a full-time job. I have no experience in part-time, and I never found a place where they will hire me. What am I missing? Is my appearance disgusting..?

Something I can now celebrate without neutral feelings. Maybe not as I expected, though.

I recently got back in the mood to draw something, and I made a small icon of vee sitting on a platform. It still feels hard for me, despite being able to draw in a rather fine quality last year.

My mind has been full of unrealistic thoughts. and the pain of being a male is striking harder than before.

There are multiple genders to choose from. I was talking with several friends of mine regarding genders, and one of them suggested me being gender-fluid - unstable status with gender swapping from one to another at times.
But I don't like to feel like "he", yet I don't want to be nothing or be like part of a group of people, so I chose to be transgender female. It was something I long wanted (to be called "she"), anyways.

I couldn't stand being a male when I have weakness of any sort. Especially when admitting to have a mental sickness.

The male stereotype of being perfect still stands strong. But female stereotypes slowly strengthens within me.

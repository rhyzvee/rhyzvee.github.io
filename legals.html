Reposts

I do not allow reposts of my contents by other people. These do not include saving pins on Pinterest, attaching links or placing embeds. I may post the same content through multiple sites, but that does not mean you can share them on your own will.

What if a content is posted on multiple media?

It is common that I “repost” the same content on multiple platforms. In this case, legals will switch to one side, and another side's would not be applied further on. Usually, this would result in restriction switching to the General level, with the exception of videos.

For instance, if an image originally posted on Instagram was uploaded on Discord, then it would be free to use with credit.

Side note with uploads on Pinterest

Pinterest has two ways of uploading pins - using embedding file from a target link, or adding files directly. Due to the nature of Pinterest's system, the former solution will not count as illegal repost. The second will, however. You will notice it when you only see the author and description below the pin's file.
